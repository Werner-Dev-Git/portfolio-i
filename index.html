03b — How it goes

Discover, design, build, ship

The same four moves on every project, whether it is a landing page or a full product surface.

/ 01

Discover

Who is arriving, what they need in the first ten seconds, and what the business needs them to do. Written down before a single frame exists.

/ 02

Design

Figma, properly: a type scale, a grid, real colour tokens and components — so the build has something to follow instead of a picture to copy.

/ 03

Build

Hand-written and responsive from the first breakpoint. Motion is added where it explains something, and it always respects reduced-motion.

/ 04

Ship

Deployed, checked on a real phone, and handed over — with the source in your hands, not locked inside a builder.
