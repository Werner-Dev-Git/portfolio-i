About

Developer first, designer second. Which is backwards — and exactly why nothing gets lost between the two.

Most of my time is in the code. I design because the build keeps asking questions — type scale, spacing, colour, how a thing should move — and answering them myself is faster than handing the question back and waiting.

So the order is inverted on paper, and it means the design has never once arrived as something that could not be built. Everything on this page is hand-written, WebGL included.

How I work

Discover, design, build, ship

The same four moves on every project, whether it is a landing page or a full product surface.

01

Discover

Who is arriving, what they need in the first ten seconds, and what the business needs them to do.

02

Design

Figma, properly — a type scale, a grid and real components, so the build has something to follow.

03

Build

Hand-written and responsive from the first breakpoint, with motion baked in rather than bolted on.

04

Ship

Deployed, checked on a real phone, handed over — source and all.
